ClickShift stays focused on reliable shifting, a quiet Mac experience, and clear controls.

ClickShift 1.2.2

September 2026
  • Added reliable reconnection after the Mac wakes from sleep.
  • Restarts Bluetooth scanning and refreshes the selected riding app automatically after wake.
  • Keeps the menu-bar icon orange while Bluetooth is recovering instead of briefly showing an error.
  • Prevents stale disconnect callbacks from interrupting a newly restored Click connection.

ClickShift 1.2.1

September 2026
  • Restored manual training-app selection in onboarding and Settings.
  • Removed automatic target switching.
  • Clarified how the trainer, Click, and keyboard shortcuts stay separate.
  • Kept profiles for MyWhoosh, TrainingPeaks Virtual, ROUVY, and custom apps.
  • Improved clickable areas throughout Settings and the menu panel.

ClickShift 1.1.0

September 2026
  • Introduced the guided setup assistant and modern Settings window.
  • Added configurable Click buttons, keys, and 1–3 shifts per press.
  • Added app-safe shortcut delivery, privacy-safe diagnostics, and meaningful notifications.
  • Added a flat, connection-aware menu-bar icon.
