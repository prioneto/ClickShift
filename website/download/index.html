macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · 811 KB

⌘ Download ZIP

Move to Applications

Unzip the download and drag ClickShift.app into your Applications folder.

Complete setup

Choose your training app and allow Bluetooth and Accessibility when prompted.

Wake and ride

Open your training app, press + or B on the right Click, and wait for green.

macOS security: ClickShift is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized. If the first launch is blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway, and confirm. Only bypass this warning for a build downloaded from the official GitHub repository.
